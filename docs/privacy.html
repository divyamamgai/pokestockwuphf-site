Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

PokeBell ("we", "our", "us") operates the PokeBell mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and collecting only the minimum data necessary to provide our service.

What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to deliver our notification service. We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, or any other personally identifiable information.

  • Anonymous Identifier — a randomly generated identifier created when you first open the app, not linked to any personal information.
  • Push Notification Token — a device-specific token used to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Subscription and Purchase Data — managed by RevenueCat on our behalf, including your subscription status, purchase history, and billing period. Payment details are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play Store.
  • Basic Device Information — platform (iOS/Android) and app version, used to ensure notifications are delivered correctly.

How We Use Your Data

  • Delivering push notifications about product stock availability.
  • Managing your subscription tier and ensuring access to the correct features.
  • Improving the reliability and performance of our notification service.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate PokeBell:

  • Firebase (Google) — provides anonymous authentication and push notification delivery. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • Your anonymous identifier and push notification token are retained for as long as your account is active and you have the app installed.
  • Subscription data is retained in accordance with RevenueCat's data retention policy.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@pokebell.co.uk. Uninstalling the app will automatically remove your push notification token, stopping all notifications. Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove your anonymous identifier and any associated data from our systems.

Children's Privacy

PokeBell is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with data, please contact us at support@pokebell.co.uk and we will promptly delete it.

International Transfers & Legal Basis

  • Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States.
  • We process your data on the basis of legitimate interests, specifically to deliver the service you requested.
  • PokeBell is operated from the United Kingdom and can be contacted at support@pokebell.co.uk.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@pokebell.co.uk.
