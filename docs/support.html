Common Topics

Getting Started

Download PokeBell from the App Store or Google Play Store. When you first open the app, the app sets up automatically — no sign-up required. You'll immediately start receiving free-tier stock alerts for products at supported retailers.

Managing Your Subscription

PokeBell Standard ($4.99/month) unlocks all stock alerts, notification rules, and custom sounds. To manage or cancel your subscription:

  • iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PokeBell.
  • Android: Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → PokeBell.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain access to Standard features until then.

Notification Troubleshooting

If you're not receiving notifications, try the following:

  • Ensure notifications are enabled for PokeBell in your device settings.
  • Check that your device is connected to the internet.
  • On Android, ensure PokeBell is excluded from battery optimisation (Settings → Apps → PokeBell → Battery → Unrestricted).
  • Try closing and reopening the app to refresh your push token.
  • If the issue persists, contact us and include your device model and OS version.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your data, email support@pokebell.co.uk with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will delete your associated data from our systems. Note that uninstalling the app automatically removes your push notification token.
