Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration — the full product lineup

The Pokémon Company has revealed the full lineup for Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration — a commemorative special expansion launching worldwide on 16 September 2026. It's an all-foil set built around the franchise's 30th anniversary, rolling out in waves through to December.

What makes the set special

  • Every card is foil — the announcement confirms the expansion is made up entirely of foil cards.
  • 30 unique Pikachu cards, with one guaranteed in every booster pack.
  • A brand-new rarity, Futuristic rare — illustrated by renowned Japanese artist YOSHIROTTEN, with vibrant new visuals. Mew and Mewtwo are among the cards revealed in the new rarity.
  • 30 classic cards return via a special three-card “Classic Collection” booster — the first time this booster type has appeared in English. The Classic Collection pack itself is exclusive to the Ultra-Premium Collections.
  • A Day & Night theme runs through the artwork, with Nidorina featured as the Elite Trainer Box promo.

Digital players can jump in a day early: the set goes live in Pokémon TCG Live on 15 September 2026 at 6 p.m. BST.

Release schedule

  • 16 September — Elite Trainer Box, Pokémon Center ETB, Poster Collection, Tech Sticker Collection, Pokémon ex Box, Knock Out Collection, 2-Pack Blister
  • 2 October — Day & Night Mini Tins, Binder Collection, Booster Bundle
  • 30 October — Espeon ex & Umbreon ex Battle Decks
  • 6 November — Ultra-Premium Collections, Ditto Premium Collection, Mewtwo & Mew Figure Collections
  • 4 December — Pokémon ex Tin

The products

Highlights from the lineup. Prices shown are the US MSRP from the announcement — UK RRPs will be confirmed closer to launch.

16 September

  • Elite Trainer Box — 9 booster packs, an illustration-rare-style Nidorina promo, 16 foil Basic Energy and accessories ($49.99).
  • Pokémon Center Elite Trainer Box — the Pokémon Center exclusive ETB variant.
  • Poster Collection — 3 boosters, Articuno / Zapdos / Moltres promos and a two-sided poster ($14.99).
  • Tech Sticker Collection — 3 boosters, a foil Alolan Exeggutor or Lucario promo and a sticker sheet ($14.99).
  • Pokémon ex Box — 4 boosters, a Sylveon ex or Greninja ex promo and an oversize card ($21.99).
  • Knock Out Collection & 2-Pack Blister — 2 boosters, a foil Eevee promo and a commemorative coin ($9.99 each).

2 October

  • Day & Night Mini Tins — 2 boosters, a sticker sheet and art card; the Unown artwork spells “ANNIVERSARY” ($9.99).
  • Binder Collection — 5 boosters and a 9-pocket portfolio ($31.99).
  • Booster Bundle — 6 booster packs ($26.94).

30 October

  • Espeon ex & Umbreon ex Battle Decks — all-foil 60-card decks with deck box, coin, playmat and an illustration-rare-style Victini or Zeraora ($19.99).

6 November

  • Ultra-Premium Collections (two variants — Pikachu ex Day + Espeon ex, or Pikachu ex Night + Umbreon ex) — 29 boosters plus a 3-card Classic Collection pack, a themed playmat, sleeves, deck box and accessories ($179.99).
  • Ditto Premium Collection — 8 boosters, a Ditto promo and an acrylic display ($39.99).
  • Mewtwo & Mew Figure Collections — 5 boosters, a foil promo plus oversize card, and a sculpted figure ($29.99).

4 December

  • Pokémon ex Tin — 4 boosters and a Sylveon ex or Greninja ex promo ($21.99).

Our take

This is a big, multi-wave anniversary set, and the Ultra-Premium Collections and Figure Collections in particular tend to sell through quickly. We'll be tracking the sealed products across UK shops as they go up for pre-order and release.
